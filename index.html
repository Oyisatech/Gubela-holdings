Experience That Transforms Businesses.

Zanele is a business transformation and enterprise development specialist with over 15 years of experience in Enterprise & Supplier Development, governance and supplier readiness. She has supported more than 50 SMMEs and managed ESD programmes with budgets over R170 million.
