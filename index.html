About Gubela Holdings

Experience That Transforms Businesses.

Zanele is a business transformation and enterprise development specialist with over 15 years of experience in Enterprise & Supplier Development, governance and supplier readiness. She has supported SMMEs across South Africa to become capable, compliant and sustainable.

She has coordinated complex projects and programmes, and spent 8 years owning an electrical construction business supplying corporates and municipalities. That owner-experience is why Gubela is practical, not theoretical.
