About Zanele Njova

Founder & Lead Business Transformation Advisor


Zanele is a business transformation and enterprise development specialist with over 15 years of experience in Enterprise & Supplier Development, governance and supplier readiness. She has supported more than 50 SMMEs and managed ESD programmes with budgets over R170 million.


She coordinated projects worth over R50 million and helped grow a supplier from R42 million to R1.2 BILLION turnover within two years at Eskom. She also spent 8 years owning an electrical construction business supplying Eskom and municipalities.


Our Mission: To transform SMMEs into capable, compliant and sustainable businesses that create jobs, build wealth and strengthen communities.
