Our six-stage approach

Assess → Diagnose → Strategise → Implement → Monitor → Sustain.

Implementation is separately scoped and priced. A coaching or advisory session does not automatically include delivery of documents, systems or operational changes.

02 / Service Matching

Choose the Right Intervention

Coaching — BUILD

Develop the founder’s business capabilities, decision-making and accountability: business model, customers, pricing, cash-flow habits and 30/60/90-day priorities.

Mentorship — GROW

Guide an operating SMME through growth decisions, leadership, delegation, profitability, operating controls, supplier readiness and KPI reviews.

Strategic advisory — TRANSFORM

Address more complex needs: strategic planning, operating models, organisational design, performance, governance, transformation and executive decision support.

Diagnostics

Paid, defined-scope assessment with findings and recommended priorities. May precede any level of engagement.

Classification rule: Business maturity and complexity—not turnover alone—determine the recommended intervention. Final fee and scope require founder approval.

06 / Process

From Enquiry to Delivery

Capture needs; book R500 paid consultation.

02 • QUALIFY

Determine maturity, urgency, budget and complexity.

03 • SCOPE

Founder approves level, quote, outputs and schedule.

04 • DEPOSIT

Collect 30% or 50% before commencement.

Provide agreed sessions or separately scoped work.

Track actions, assess progress and renew or close.

30% deposit: 40% at agreed milestone and final 30% before delivery, unless otherwise approved in writing.

07 / Delivery

Detailed Service Menu

BUILD — Business coaching

Business model, value proposition, customer discovery, pricing, sales goals, basic budgets, cash-flow routines, compliance checklist, pitch preparation and founder action plans.

GROW — Business mentorship

Growth strategy, margin and cash-flow review, process controls, delegation, management routines, leadership mentoring, KPIs, supplier onboarding and tender-readiness guidance.

08 / Boundaries

Implementation & Partner Support

Separately scoped implementation

Business plans, operating procedures, policy drafting, financial models, KPI dashboards, supplier assessments, stakeholder engagement, process redesign, training coordination and project delivery.

Specialist collaborations

Gubela may propose complementary technology, SHEQ, cybersecurity, training, IT or other specialist support through partners, subject to confirmed commercial arrangements and delivery capacity.

Corporate ESD support

Potential activities include SMME diagnostics, coaching, mentorship, supplier readiness and agreed programme support. Portfolio scale, tools, reporting, staffing and funding must be established before commitments are made.

Never promise: Guaranteed funding, contracts, sales growth, unlimited access, proprietary platforms or capacity not yet confirmed.

  • R500 consultation payable in full before booking is confirmed.
  • All diagnostics, packages and projects require an approved 30% or 50% deposit before work starts.

  • Corporate relationships remain founder-led unless delegated.
